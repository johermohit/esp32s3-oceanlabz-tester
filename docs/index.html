OceanLabz ESP32-S3 board tester

Flash the test firmware, then poke the OLED, mic, amp and TFT from your browser. Chrome or Edge only (Web Serial).

1 · Flash

Pick the board's UART/serial port (CH340 / CP210x), not the native USB one. If it won't connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.

2 · Test

Not connected.
Serial is 115200 baud. The I2C scan takes about a minute and lists every SDA/SCL pair that answers. 0x3C is the OLED.
